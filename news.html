Privacy Policy

This site collects nothing about you. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no session recording and no fingerprinting. Nothing you do here is logged, stored or profiled by us.

Every file the page loads — the stylesheet, the script, the typefaces and the photography — is served from this site's own domain. No request is made to any third party, so no third party learns that you visited.

The site is published with GitHub Pages, which like any web host receives the requests your browser makes and may keep its own server logs. That is outside our control and we have no access to them.

The newsletter field is not connected to anything: submitting it reports that nothing was recorded, because nothing was. Links to other organisations' sites are ordinary links — once you follow one, that site's own policies apply.

There is nothing to set. This site uses no cookies at all — not for analytics, not for advertising, not for preferences, not even the “strictly necessary” kind.

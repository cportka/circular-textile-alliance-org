Knowledge for a Changing Textile Economy

CTA publishes research, technical guidance, educational resources, and practical tools designed to improve understanding and support informed decision-making.

Our publications translate complex issues into accessible information for the people and organizations working throughout the textile economy.

Our Publications May Include

01

Research & Reports

Independent research and analysis examining the challenges, opportunities, and systems shaping the textile economy.

02

Technical Guidance

Practical resources designed to help organizations understand emerging issues, implement new approaches, and navigate complex textile systems.

03

Educational Resources

Clear, accessible materials that help consumers, industry professionals, educators, policymakers, and other stakeholders better understand textiles and the textile value chain.

04

Implementation Resources

Tools, frameworks, and guidance that support the practical implementation of legislation, industry initiatives, and collaborative solutions.

05

Industry Insights

Articles, perspectives, and expert contributions that explore emerging developments and important questions across the textile economy.

Our Approach to Knowledge

CTA believes reliable information is a foundation for better decision-making.

Our publications are intended to support learning, encourage constructive dialogue, and provide practical information that can help stakeholders move from awareness to action.

As CTA's work expands, our publication library will grow to include research, technical resources, educational tools, collaborative findings, and other resources that support our mission.
